# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Vite, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide React, Leaflet, Google Maps.

## Users

Madrid commuters, residents, and visitors who need immediate, reliable public transit information on mobile (specifically iPhone 14 PWA) while navigating the city.

## Product Purpose

Provide instantaneous, accurate real-time arrival estimates and line navigation for both EMT Madrid buses and Metro de Madrid through official teleindicadores and GPS positioning.

## Positioning

The fastest, cleanest, non-bloated Madrid transit companion: official live countdowns without ads, corporate clutter, or AI-generated visual slop, built to feel like a first-party native iOS app.

## Operating Context

Used on-the-go with one hand on an iPhone 14: walking down the street, waiting at a bus shelter, descending into underground metro stations under variable network connectivity and lighting conditions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dual mode: EMT Bus (stops, lines, GPS approximations) and Metro de Madrid (242 stations, platforms, line badges, official teleindicadores).
- PWA with standalone display mode, safe area compliance (notch, Dynamic Island, home bar), and offline resilience.
- Sub-second live search across 4,933 bus stops and 242 metro stations.

## Brand Commitments

- Authentic transport authority identity: Metro de Madrid Red (`#D31027`) and EMT Madrid Blue (`#0055A5`).
- Native iOS Human Interface Guidelines (SF Pro typography, grouped inset lists, standard bottom sheets, 44pt touch targets).

## Product Principles

1. Task First: Real-time arrivals in 2 taps or less.
2. Zero Slop: No marketing fluff, no nested card clutter, no fake decorative AI glows.
3. Ergonomic Mobility: Optimized for one-handed thumb navigation on modern iPhones.
4. Glancibility: Route numbers and minutes must be readable at an arm's length in bright daylight.
